# EVPark Design Guidelines

## Brand
- Name: EVPark / evpark.by
- Industry: B2B EV fleet-as-a-service, Belarus
- Vibe: Premium tech, corporate trust, eco-modern

## Colors
- Background: #0A0A0A (near black)
- Card/Surface: #141414 / #1A1A1A
- Border: #2A2A2A
- Primary Accent: #3DDC84 (bright green)
- Accent Hover: #2BC870
- Text Primary: #FFFFFF
- Text Secondary: #A0A0A0
- Text Muted: #666666

## Typography
- Font: Inter (Google Fonts)
- Hero: 56px bold / 700
- H2: 36px 700
- H3: 22px 600
- Body: 16px 400
- Small: 14px 400

## Spacing
- Section padding: 96px vertical
- Container: max-w-6xl centered

## Components
- Buttons: rounded-full, green fill or ghost outline
- Cards: dark surface with subtle border, rounded-2xl
- Badge/chip: small green-tinted label
- Inputs: dark fill, green focus ring

## Language
- Dual language: RU + BY (toggle in header)
- Default: RU
